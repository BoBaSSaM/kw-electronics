Create a complete, modern, responsive e-commerce website for a Kuwait-based electronics and hardware components company.

The website should sell products such as:

- Arduino boards
- Raspberry Pi products
- Sensors
- Electronic modules
- Integrated circuits and ICs
- Resistors, capacitors and transistors
- Breadboards and jumper wires
- Motors and motor drivers
- Power supplies
- Batteries and battery holders
- Connectors
- Cables
- Soldering equipment
- Multimeters and measurement tools
- Robotics components
- DIY electronics kits
- Electrical tools
- Computer and hardware accessories

The general concept should be similar to a professional electronics components store such as RTC Electronics Kuwait, but create an original design and branding rather than copying another website.

DESIGN STYLE

Create a clean, premium, modern technology-store appearance.

Use this default color palette:

Primary color: #0B3D91 — deep technology blue  
Secondary color: #146CFF — bright blue  
Accent color: #FFB703 — orange/yellow for important buttons, discounts and highlights  
Background: #F7F9FC  
Cards: #FFFFFF  
Primary text: #172033  
Secondary text: #667085  

Use subtle gradients where appropriate.

Use rounded cards, clean spacing, modern typography, soft shadows and professional product photography placeholders.

The design should feel like a combination of an electronics engineering supplier and a modern online technology store.

Do not make the interface overly colorful or childish.
